Hollowhold privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Hollowhold is a puzzle and strategy game by Standard Mending Co. (Kevin Ramdath). This policy covers the Hollowhold app on Android and the web.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Hollowhold has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect your name, contacts, location or anything you do in the game, and we do not sell or share personal information. The only thing that leaves your device is the update check described below, and, if you buy something, the purchase itself, which Google Play handles.

What stays on your device

Your progress, charters, Guild studies, Daily Survey bests, settings and any run in progress are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Game updates

When the app starts, it checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for a newer version of the game's content. That request carries technical details needed to deliver the update, such as the app version, platform and update channel, plus a random install identifier that the update service creates the first time the app runs. The identifier is not linked to you or your device's hardware; it lets the service roll updates out gradually and count how many installs run each version. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The request does not include your name, contacts, location or anything you do in the game. Expo's own privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

Purchases

If the app offers a purchase and you make one, Google Play processes it under Google's privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the app only learns from Google Play that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can unlock the game and restore it on your other devices.

Children

Hollowhold does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
